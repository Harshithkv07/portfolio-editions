About

My name is Harshith. I’m a developer, and the founder of Metanoia, a software company that builds apps, websites and AI features for other businesses, and ships a few products of its own.

I study Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. My work sits on both sides of that degree. On one side is research, like teaching graph neural networks to predict how particles behave inside glass. On the other are products, like PGPilot, which runs a paying-guest hostel from a single screen.
