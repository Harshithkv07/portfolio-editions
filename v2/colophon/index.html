Chapter V

Colophon

How this edition was made.

This is the second edition of this site. The first was three colours, one typeface and a dithering pipeline. This one is a book.

Type

One family throughout: the Fell types, seventeenth-century type that Bishop John Fell left to Oxford, in Igino Marini’s digital revival. Titles, contents and labels are IM Fell English small capitals; paragraphs and italics are the sturdier IM Fell DW Pica. None of it comes in bold, so nothing here is bold.

Ink

One red, on near-black. By daylight, the same red is deepened onto parchment. One chapter, Metanoia, is printed the other way round, by daylight while the rest is by candlelight, and only its titles are red.

Paper

The grain is not a picture. It is noise, drawn fresh by your browser from a few lines of SVG, and it stays still while everything above it moves.

Mechanism

Take hold of the edge of your browser window and drag it. The page stretches with you, and springs back when you let go.

The Cover

A first visit begins with the book closed. Break the wax seal, and the cover catches fire where it was and burns away to the title page.

Tabs

On a wide screen the chapters are cut into the right-hand edge, like the thumb index of a dictionary. The tabs stay still when the page stretches.

Built With

Plain HTML, one stylesheet and one small script, with a single tiny server function that carries the drop box to Discord. No framework and no build step.

Set, stretched, and sent out in the year 2026
